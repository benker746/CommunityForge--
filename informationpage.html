Information about CommunityForge

Welcome to CommunityForge, a community-driven platform where you can borrow items and learn new skills completely for free. Instead of spending money on expensive tools or costly professional lessons, this platform helps save money while building meaningful connections with our neighbors.

Options Available as per below:
  • Listings Page: Browse all available skills and items in your area. If someone wants to borrow your item or learn your skill, you will receive an automatic email notification. You can also have a live viewing of how many people saw your listing. As well can save it.
  • Skills to share: Use the Skill Page to share the list of knowledge or talent that you are excited to mentor.
  • Items ready to lend: Use the Item Page to list the things you are willing to lend.
  • Tips: While everything on CommunityForge is free to access, you are welcome to coordinate with your peer and leave a voluntary tip of your choice to show your appreciation. At its core, CommunityForge is all about saving money and making friends.

Thank you for joining us and helping in making a positive impact to our community!
